Not everything worth shipping is a product. A running collection of small, sharp tools, each
built because something was annoying that day:

- **compare_excel** — diffs two spreadsheets and writes out the rows that exist in only one of
  them. The kind of thing a colleague does by hand for three hours, twice a year.
- **TakeScreenshot** — headless page capture via Selenium, from before that was a one-liner.
- **MetadataAnalyzer** — reads what files quietly carry around with them.
- **slack-workspace-cleaner** — because workspaces accumulate.
- **image-upload-api** — a deliberately boring Node upload endpoint.
- **advent_of_code_23** — December, in Python, badly, happily.
- **url-redirector-fastapi**, **Expense-Tracking**, **Simple-Blog**, **CRM** — clean small
  builds in whatever stack I wanted to keep sharp that month, including PHP, which more of the
  world runs on than admits it.

They stay public for two reasons. Small complete programs teach better than large incomplete
ones. And a public commit history going back to 2017 is a more honest CV than anything I could
write about myself.
